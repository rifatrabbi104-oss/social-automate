Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to SocialAutomate ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our automation and marketing application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect your social media accounts (including TikTok and WhatsApp).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information strictly necessary to provide scheduling and customer support automation services:

  • Social Media Credentials: When you connect through official OAuth (e.g., TikTok Login Kit or WhatsApp Cloud API), we receive and securely store authorization access tokens and creator/user IDs. We do NOT store your account passwords.
  • Product & E-Commerce Data: Product titles, descriptions, price, and images you upload to schedule posts.
  • Customer Communication Logs: Inquiries sent by customers to your WhatsApp or social channels to facilitate automated AI responses and live agent handover.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Publishing scheduled marketing video and image content directly to your authorized TikTok or social channels via official APIs (e.g., TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Processing and answering inbound customer inquiries using automated assistants.
  • Providing analytics and real-time delivery status in your private administrative dashboard.

3. Data Storage & Security

All authentication tokens and operational data are stored securely within isolated Cloudflare D1 databases utilizing industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We never share, sell, or rent your data to any third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services & APIs

Our service integrates with official third-party APIs:

  • TikTok for Developers: We comply with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Developer Privacy Policy. Content uploaded through the app adheres strictly to TikTok Community Guidelines.
  • Meta / WhatsApp: Used for automated messaging and customer notifications.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may disconnect your social accounts at any time from your settings dashboard. To request permanent deletion of all stored credentials and message history, please email us with your user account identifier.
