Facebook User Data Deletion Instructions

SocialAutomate respects your privacy and gives you full control over your data. According to Meta Platform rules, you have the right to request deletion of any data associated with your Facebook account.

How to Delete Your Data:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy ➜ Settings.
  2. Click on Apps and Websites to see all applications connected to your Facebook account.
  3. Search for SocialAutomate or the connected automation app.
  4. Click Remove. This immediately revokes all access permissions.
  5. To request permanent deletion of any cached credentials or logs stored in our Cloudflare D1 database, send an email to rifathassanrabbi103@gmail.com with your Facebook Page ID. All records will be permanently purged within 24 hours.

For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
